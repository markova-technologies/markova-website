Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Markova's Amharic voice capabilities unique?

Most commercial voice AI platforms fail on African languages due to lack of training data. Markova builds fine-tuned acoustic representations and phoneme mappings for Amharic, delivering natural pronunciation and high recognition accuracy.

Can we keep our current phone numbers?

Yes. Markova Voice agents connect via SIP forwarding or telephony API webhooks, allowing businesses to retain their established support lines and toll-free numbers.

How are human escalations managed?

When a call requires human judgment or falls outside policy boundaries, the agent executes a warm transfer to a human specialist, providing them with a 3-bullet summary of the caller's issue.
