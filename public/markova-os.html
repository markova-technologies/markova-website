Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Markova OS and regular AI tools?

Most AI software works as passive conversational assistants that produce text when queried. Markova OS is an operational platform where agents actively execute business processes across CRMs, ERPs, and databases under strict audit controls.

Can we test Markova OS before enterprise rollout?

Yes. Markova offers pilot deployments tailored to specific departmental workflows (such as customer support automation or data harmonization) allowing organizations to measure ROI and accuracy before scaling.

How do we integrate our existing internal systems?

Markova Neural Fabric connectors interface directly with REST/GraphQL APIs, webhooks, SQL databases, and internal spreadsheets without requiring organizations to replace their established software infrastructure.
