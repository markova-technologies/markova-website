The Markova Operating Model

AI that understands the business,
not just the conversation.

A normal AI assistant answers questions. Markova understands business context, reasons about what needs to happen, acts inside business systems, coordinates AI workers, and remains accountable to humans.

01 · Understand 02 · Think 03 · Act 04 · Work Together 05 · Stay Accountable
01

UNDERSTAND

Markova learns how the business works.

Markova connects to the company's knowledge, CRM, calendar, and internal systems. The AI does not only hear what a customer says—it understands the business context behind the conversation.

Example

Customer: "I want to know if my order is ready."

Markova understands: Who the customer is, which order they refer to, the order status, the policy, and what action should happen next.

02

THINK

Markova decides what should happen next.

Markova does not rely on one generic AI agent for everything. A Commander can reason about the situation and route work to the right AI specialist (Sales, Support, Operations, Finance, Scheduling).

03

ACT

Markova does more than answer.

The conversation is only the beginning. The work happens after understanding. Markova can take real business actions like booking appointments, checking availability, updating CRM records, and creating tickets.

04

WORK TOGETHER

One business. A workforce of AI specialists.

This stage is a live, interactive experience. Watch the Commander AI coordinate Sales, Support, and Operations agents in real time across a real business scenario.

Launch Live Visualization
05

STAY ACCOUNTABLE

AI that works with humans, not around them.

Autonomous when it can be. Accountable when it matters. Businesses need visibility and control. Humans can monitor live AI activity, review conversations, approve sensitive actions, and intervene when necessary.
