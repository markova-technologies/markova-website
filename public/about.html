ABOUT MARKOVA TECHNOLOGY

Intelligence That Works for Everyone

Markova is an AI technology company building an agentic ecosystem where intelligent software, autonomous workers, and human teams collaborate seamlessly.

Bridging Today's Operations and Tomorrow's AI Workforce

Artificial intelligence is rapidly entering every company, yet most implementations remain isolated: one chatbot answers simple FAQs, another sends canned emails, and a third runs basic macros. Organizations are left managing fragmented tools rather than cohesive systems that actually perform work.

Markova was established with a singular objective: to help businesses adopt real AI workers without forcing them to abandon their legacy software, spreadsheets, or existing workflows.

Headquartered in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, and serving enterprise clients globally, Markova combines world-class agentic orchestration with localized multilingual capabilities, including state-of-the-art voice models for Amharic and African languages.

Our Guiding Principle
"Technology should simplify work, unlock human potential, and quietly power business progress from behind the scenes."

Core Values

Human-Centered

AI should amplify human capability, not alienate teams. We engineer systems that work alongside staff with clear accountability and human oversight.

Security by Design

Enterprise data is sovereign. We enforce strict data sandboxing, role-based access control, zero public model training on client data, and audit logging.

Enterprise Reliability

Business operations demand deterministic execution. Our multi-agent swarms use continuous validation loops to prevent hallucinations and execution errors.
